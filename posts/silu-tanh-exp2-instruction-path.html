SiLU 的 tanh 形式与 exp2 形式: 指令路径比较

SiLU 的数学定义简单,但 kernel 不执行公式,它执行编译后的指令。sigmoid 写法通常会落到 ex2 加 reciprocal/divide;tanh 恒等式则把倒数那段换成几条 FMUL/FFMA。这只在 activation 本身是热点时值得讨论。如果 kernel 已经被 memory、load/store 或 tensor core GEMM 限住,换公式可能只会改变局部指令统计。

1. 公式关系

SiLU 定义为:

\[ \mathrm{silu}(x)=x\sigma(x),\qquad \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}} \]

在 NVIDIA GPU 上,指数路径常被改写成 base-2 exponential。PTX 里有 ex2.approx.f32,所以 sigmoid 可以写成:

sigmoid / exp2

\[ \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+2^{-x\log_2 e}} \]

tanh identity

\[ \sigma(x)=\frac{1+\tanh(x/2)}{2} \]

两个公式在实数域等价。到了 GPU 指令层,它们已经不是同一个实现:一个走 ex2 后接 reciprocal 或 divide,另一个走 tanh 后接普通浮点乘加。

2. 指令路径

下面按 FP32 scalar 路径估算。真实 SASS 取决于编译器、fast math 选项、目标架构和 dtype。

3. 性能直觉

CUDA Best Practices Guide 的 native arithmetic throughput 表把 FP32 add/multiply/FMA 和 approximate reciprocal/ex2/log/sin/cos 放在不同吞吐档位。以常见现代架构的表项看,普通 FP32 乘加每个 SM 每 cycle 的理论结果数高于 approximate special-function 那一类。具体比例随架构变,但性能假设可以落到一条指令链上:多付几条 FMUL/FFMA,换掉 sigmoid 路径里的 reciprocal/divide。

因果不要写反。tanh 版本的优势来源不是 MUFU.TANH 必然快过 MUFU.EX2。PTX 文档只定义 tanh.approx.f32 和 ex2.approx.f32 的语义、精度和架构要求,没有承诺所有 GPU 上 tanh 对 ex2 的固定倍率。更保守的说法是:exp2 sigmoid 路径通常还要做倒数或除法,tanh 恒等式把这段换成了高吞吐的乘加。

半精度路径还要看 dtype。PTX 支持 tanh.approx.f16、tanh.approx.f16x2、tanh.approx.bf16 和 tanh.approx.bf16x2,其中 bf16/bf16x2 的目标架构要求更高。kernel 如果在 half2 或 bf16x2 上跑,必须确认编译结果有没有生成 packed instruction。否则纸面指令数只是源码层面的猜测。

4. 编译与验证

这类改写不能只看源码。判断时看这几层:

  • 用 cuobjdump --dump-sass 或 nvdisasm 看 cubin,确认最终是 MUFU.EX2、MUFU.TANH、RCP、FFMA,还是 libdevice 调用。
  • 固定编译选项再比较,尤其是 -use_fast_math、-ftz、-prec-div。这些选项会改变除法、denormal 和 intrinsic lowering。
  • 单独测数值误差。tanh identity 和 exp2 sigmoid 在实数域等价,但近似指令、flush-to-zero 和 rounding 会让结果不再 bitwise 相同。

比较保守的结论是:当 SiLU 是热点,并且目标架构能把 tanh lowering 成近似硬件指令时,tanh 恒等式通常比 exp2 加 reciprocal 的 sigmoid 路径更值得试。它是一个优化候选,不是定理。结果以 SASS 和 profiling 为准。

SiLU via tanh vs exp2: instruction-path comparison

SiLU has a compact definition, but a kernel executes instructions, not algebra. The sigmoid form often becomes ex2 plus reciprocal/divide. The tanh identity trades that reciprocal path for a few FMUL/FFMA instructions. This matters only when the activation itself is hot. If the kernel is limited by memory traffic, load/store pressure, or tensor core GEMM, the change may only move local instruction counters.

1. Formula

\[ \mathrm{silu}(x)=x\sigma(x),\qquad \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}} \]

sigmoid / exp2

\[ \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+2^{-x\log_2 e}} \]

tanh identity

\[ \sigma(x)=\frac{1+\tanh(x/2)}{2} \]

The two forms are equivalent over real numbers. At the GPU instruction level, they are different implementations: the exp2 form still needs a reciprocal or divide, while the tanh form adds ordinary floating-point multiply-add work around one approximate tanh.

3. Performance intuition

The CUDA Best Practices Guide puts ordinary FP32 add/multiply/FMA and approximate reciprocal/ex2/log/sin/cos in different throughput classes. On common modern compute capabilities, FP32 multiply-add has higher theoretical result throughput per SM per cycle than the approximate special-function class. The ratio depends on the GPU. The performance hypothesis is narrower: spend a few FMUL/FFMA instructions to remove reciprocal/divide from the sigmoid path.

The tanh form should not be justified by assuming one MUFU.TANH is always faster than one MUFU.EX2. NVIDIA's PTX ISA defines both approximate instructions and their target requirements, but the final schedule is a hardware and compiler question. The part worth testing is the removal of reciprocal/divide.

4. Build and validation

  • Inspect SASS with cuobjdump --dump-sass or nvdisasm.
  • Compare with fixed compiler flags, especially -use_fast_math, -ftz, and -prec-div.
  • Measure numerical error separately. Approximate instructions, flush-to-zero behavior, and rounding mean the two formulas do not have to match bit for bit.

If SiLU is hot and the target architecture lowers tanh to an approximate hardware instruction, the tanh identity is usually the better candidate to try. It is an optimization candidate, not a theorem. The answer still has to come from SASS and profiling.
